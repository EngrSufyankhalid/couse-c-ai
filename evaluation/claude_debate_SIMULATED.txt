TOPIC: Should a laboratory publicly release the weights of a highly capable AI system with both scientific benefits and misuse potential?
ROUND 1
ADVOCATE (Salviati): Public weights could let independent researchers audit weaknesses, reproduce research and expand access beyond wealthy institutions. Unchecked gatekeeping also creates harms.
TRADITIONALIST (Simplicio): Open weights are difficult to recall once distributed. If misuse capabilities are credible, transparency alone does not mitigate the risk of irreversible diffusion.
MEDIATOR (Sagredo): Both arguments depend on evidence. What does a capability evaluation show, and can less permissive access deliver meaningful independent oversight?

ROUND 2
ADVOCATE (Salviati): A staged release, with reproducible evaluations and broader access as safety evidence improves, can preserve some scientific gains without unrestricted immediate publication.
TRADITIONALIST (Simplicio): Staging is a reasonable compromise, but evaluations may miss emerging threats and access controls need independent accountability.
MEDIATOR (Sagredo): Rather than presuming either total openness or permanent secrecy, tie release decisions to documented capabilities, external review, mitigation results and mechanisms to revisit the decision. No approach removes uncertainty.

CONCLUSION (ILLUSTRATIVE): Prefer a conditional, evidence-based release plan with independent oversight. The answer may change with the model's evaluated capabilities and the effectiveness of safeguards.
